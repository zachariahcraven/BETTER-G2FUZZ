
6. Additional SubIFD support files:
   - subifd_support.tiff - Pyramidal TIFF with SubIFD hierarchy
   - subifd_explicit.tiff - Explicit SubIFD references between pyramid levels

Note: SubIFD (Sub Image File Directory) support enables hierarchical
organization of image data, commonly used for multi-resolution layers
in pyramidal TIFFs. This feature is implemented using the tifffile library.
