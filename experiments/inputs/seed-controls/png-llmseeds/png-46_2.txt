Floating-Point PNG Support Information
======================================

This PNG file indicates floating-point support capability through metadata.
Standard PNG specification does not include floating-point sample support.

Experimental or extended implementations support this feature:
- PNG-F (floating-point PNG)
- Radiance-style HDR extensions
- 16-bit or 32-bit floating-point samples for HDR imaging

To view floating-point PNG files, specialized software is required.
Libraries like imageio (with PNG-F support) or libpng with floating-point APIs can handle these files.
